## Independent review and corrections

An independent read-only reviewer identified twelve material gaps. Corrections
have regression coverage: redirect validation without forwarding POST bodies;
retaining destination restrictions until sensitive documents are destroyed;
rejecting unrelated employer redirects; corroborating Easy Apply company identity;
atomic queue actions; pause checks before individual browser actions; conservative
country-specific authorization; description residency constraints; ambiguous
cross-listing duplicates; current approval/CV activation at submission; positive
identification of resume attachments and refusal of signatures; and a new visible
confirmation after the submit attempt. Pause/resume preserves explicit approvals,
while profile or search changes invalidate them.

The bounded follow-up review found three further issues, now covered and fixed:
qualified or negative sponsorship statements remain unknown/false, multiple
salary ranges remain unknown, and every fetched browser response is disposed
after fulfillment or rejection to avoid retained bodies during continuous runs.

## Initial scope limits

- Live authenticated LinkedIn selectors and real employer submissions still need
  testing with the user's account. Discovery reads currently rendered search
  results; exhaustive pagination is deferred.
- Greenhouse/Lever currently share the standard HTML engine. Site-specific
  widgets, embedded forms, account creation, multi-domain SSO and assessments
  require subsequent adapters. Unknown required facts and CAPTCHA/MFA remain
  attention items. No challenge bypass is implemented.
- Employer corroboration reduces risk but cannot establish that every listing is
  real or actively hiring. Missing evidence keeps applications pending.
- The worker runs while the launcher, computer and network remain available.
  OS startup registration and sleep prevention are not configured.
- Work remains directly in the requested directory; no Git branch, commit or
  remote publication was created because this directory has no Git repository.
